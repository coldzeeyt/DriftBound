DRIFTBOUND

a sideways-scrolling reflex game

Space / Click / Tap to jump · Esc to pause

How to Play

Bolt

Press to jump. Hold to jump higher.

Pulsar

Hold to charge, release to launch.

Comet

Tap to boost, glide floatily between taps.

Anchor

Press to arc-leap to the opposite surface.

Phase

Hold/release steers momentum, not direction. Touch nothing.

Yellow orbs give a mid-air boost when you press near them. Pink pads bounce you automatically. Portals switch your mode or flip gravity. Reach the flag to win — or race the clock forever in Endless Mode.
